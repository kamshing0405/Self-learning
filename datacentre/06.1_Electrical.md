# Electrical Concepts

## Basic Concepts

1. `Energy` is the ability to do work

2. `Power` is the rate at which energy is consumed(how fast work gets done)

3. P = E/t (Energy/Time)

## Ohm's Law

1. Voltage (V), Current (I), Resistance (R)

- V = I × R

2. Concept by water flow

- Water will flow at a given rate dependent on the open faucet
- Water pressure(`Voltage`) usually remains constant
- Current is controlled by the faucet position(`Resistance`), can also be controlled by an increase or loss of water pressure(`Voltage`)
- `Materials` with higher `resistance` require more voltage to make the electricity flow

3. Rule of electricity

- An imcomplete loop will not have flowing electricity
- Current is the flow of electrons in the `same direction` and is muasured in Ampleres(amps or A)
- Voltage is the `pressure` that causes electrons to `flow` and is measured in volts(V)

4. The Power Equation
   `P(Watts) = V(Volts) × I(Amps)`

## DC and AC

DC (Direct Current)

- A flow of electrons in one direction（The One-Way Street）
- Often utilized with telecom loads
- Source: Batteries, solar panels, and USB ports.
- 12V, 24V, 48V

AC (Alternating Current)

- A flow of electrons in both directions （The Wave）
- Combination of voltage and current
- AC voltage can enable power to be distributed over long distances with minimal losses
- freqencies can range between 50Hz or 60Hz
- 120V, 208V, 240V
- 1 phase or 3 phase (120 degrees)
- 1 phase: Single phase AC power is a type of electrical power that is delivered to a single circuit or load. It is the most common type of electrical power used in homes and small businesses.
- 3 phase: Three-phase AC power is a type of electrical power that is delivered to three circuits or loads. It is the most common type of electrical power used in large businesses and industrial facilities.

Power Units

- kW (kilowatt) are units of power while kilowatt-hours are units of energy.
