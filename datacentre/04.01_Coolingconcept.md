## Issues of cooling

1. Short cycling

- common cause of overheating.
- It occurs when cold air from the cooling unit (CRAC/CRAH) returns to the intake of the cooling unit without ever passing through the IT equipment.

## Refrigerant cycle

- Compression（The refrigerant is compressed to a `higher pressure` and temperature）
- Condensation（The refrigerant is cooled and liquefied）
- Expansion（a sudden "flash evaporation." The rapid drop in pressure causes the temperature to plummet）
- Evaporation（cold refrigerant absorbs heat from the food/air, causing the liquid to "boil" and turn back into a gas.）
